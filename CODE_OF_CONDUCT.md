# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. Everyone is welcome here regardless of who they are or
where they're from.

Keep discussion focused on the work — the code, the diagnostics, the
parity gap. Disagreement is fine and useful; personal attacks,
harassment, insults, and unwelcome personal or sexual attention are
not.

Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are two people looking at
different parts of the same overlay.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments and block accounts that
violate this, at their discretion.

Report problems privately to harshsinghmandan@gmail.com. Reports are
handled confidentially.
